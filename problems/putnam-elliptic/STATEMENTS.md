# Three problems inspired by elliptic curves

No knowledge of elliptic curves is assumed.

## 1. Two zeros

Let \(a,b\) be real numbers, not both zero. Prove that
\[
t^4-6at^2+8bt-3a^2
\]
has exactly two distinct real zeros.

## 2. Counts that do not change

Let \(p>3\) be a prime. For an integer \(a\) and each
\(b\in\{0,1,\ldots,p-1\}\), let \(N_a(b)\) be the number of
ordered pairs \((x,y)\in\{0,1,\ldots,p-1\}^2\) satisfying
\[
y^2\equiv x^3+ax+b\pmod p.
\]
Determine all residue classes \(a\) modulo \(p\) for which
\[
N_a(0)=N_a(1)=\cdots=N_a(p-1).
\]

## 3. Squares at every integer

Let \(P\) be a polynomial with real coefficients. Suppose that, for
every positive integer \(n\), the number \(P(n)^3+1\) is the square
of an integer. Prove that \(P\) is constant.
